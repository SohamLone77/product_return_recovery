Knapsack Selection Formula: Incremental Repair Benefit = Net Repair Recovery - Best Feasible Non-Repair Net Recovery

Slots are awarded in descending order of incremental benefit. If a device has negative incremental benefit (e.g. Budget Phone at -₹33.50) or is strictly dominated by a non-repair route (e.g. Smartwatch Refurbish at +₹160.60), the optimizer will NEVER allocate a bench slot to it, even if slots are empty.

Economic Optimization Rationale

The optimization engine delivers substantial financial outperformance because it solves the combinatorial knapsack problem over opportunity costs. Naive FIFO repair blindly consumes bench slots on low-margin items. The cheapest-repair heuristic wastes slots on low-repair-cost budget items that yield trivial resale value, while leaving high-value hardware to depreciate.

Automated Routing Blocked by Policy Guardrails. The decision engine strictly halts automatic routing whenever authenticity risk exceeds 70% or physical safety hazards (swollen batteries) are detected. These items must be physically verified by a senior technician or legal specialist before disposition.
